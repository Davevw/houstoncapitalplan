5.Retail Marketing & Reconfiguration

The retail reconfiguration strategy from June remains in place. July did not change the core premise: concentrate retail at stronger intersection locations and avoid over-allocating weaker internal frontage to retail uses. The continued capital and buyer outreach in July supports keeping the site plan flexible across multifamily, industrial/flex, retail pads, and mixed-use alternatives.

  • Retail remains intersection-led - strongest value still belongs at visible corner/intersection pad locations.
  • Interior locations remain flexible - internal corridor parcels can shift toward multifamily, flex, light industrial, or other uses with stronger demand.
  • Buyer feedback drives absorption - the July outreach process should continue to inform lot configuration and pricing assumptions.
July Positioning

July did not require a format change to the retail strategy. The project should continue presenting a flexible master plan that allows capital partners and purchasers to underwrite several use mixes while preserving the highest-value retail pads for the most visible frontage and intersection locations.
